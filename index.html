FARMRAID

Grow Your Farm. Defend Your Harvest. Build Your Legacy.

Experience the life of a farmer where every seed matters and every harvest is worth protecting. Cultivate your land, defend your crops from pest raids, and discover the challenges and rewards of farming.

An educational farm sim with something hungry in the field

FARMRAID is a farming simulation that blends resource management, agricultural awareness and pest defense. You plant, water and harvest, but you also have to learn what threatens your crops and how real farmers deal with it.

Purpose
Make everyday farming knowledge fun to learn through play.
Objective
Grow a thriving farm, protect every harvest and unlock milestones as it expands.
What's different
Most farming games let crops grow in peace. In FARMRAID the pests come for them.

The Pest Raid system

At any time, pests can raid your fields. A raid is your cue to act fast.

  1. Spot the warning on your farm.
  2. Identify the pest, with help from Manong.
  3. Choose the right defense and protect the crops.
  4. Keep your harvest, or lose some of it.

Everything a farm needs

Eight systems that work together, from the first seed to a full-grown farm.

Meet the characters

You, the farmer

Create your own farmer and take over an empty plot of land. Everything that grows here is yours to protect.

Manong

A friendly Filipino farmer-inspired AI assistant. He can help with:

  • Planting instructions
  • Crop-growing tips and farming techniques
  • Pest identification and prevention
  • Agricultural knowledge

How to play

  1. Create your farmer character.
  2. Prepare your farming land.
  3. Plant and grow your crops.
  4. Monitor your farm for pest attacks.
  5. Protect your crops and harvest them.
  6. Earn resources and improve your farm.
  7. Expand your agricultural journey.
